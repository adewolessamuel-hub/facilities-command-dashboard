## What happened
**Preventive work slipped.** Preventive share fell to 28.9%, six points below September and about ten below the 12-month average. October is the lowest month in the dataset. The three sites sat between 27% and 31%, so this is portfolio-wide, not one site.

**Response time drifted up.** Average response rose from 10.3 to 11.8 hours. Bramalea was slowest at 13.0 hours; Steeles was quickest at 10.3. The slowdown is in routine (P3: 8.1 to 9.4 hrs) and low-priority (P4: 22.0 to 25.4 hrs) work; urgent (P2) response improved to 1.5 hrs. There were no P1 emergencies in October (12-month P1 average: 0.6 hrs).

**Cost fell, but read it carefully.** October cost is $49,206, down 31% on September. Two things flatter this figure: volume was lower (83 orders against 109), and 24 October orders are still open and carry only partial cost. Expect the month to rise as they close. HVAC was the largest cost line again at $16,794, followed by Plumbing at $9,119.

**Backlog.** 49 orders are open across the portfolio (Bramalea 18, Heartlake 18, Steeles 13). Eight are P1 or P2 and should be cleared first.

## Actions for November
1. Clear the eight open P1/P2 orders and review why any are older than their priority target.
2. Rebuild the preventive schedule at Bramalea (29% in October, 33% over 12 months) and restore the 40% portfolio level.
3. Review Bramalea's routine-order dispatch to pull response time back under 11 hours.
4. Re-run the cost comparison once October orders close, before drawing conclusions.
